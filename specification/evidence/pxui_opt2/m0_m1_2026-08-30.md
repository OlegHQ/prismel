## M1 layout snapshot candidate

The candidate retains one authoritative layout snapshot containing displayed
rows, indexed row/control bounds, content height, panel height, and scroll
range. Layout-affecting updates invalidate it explicitly. Indexed widget update
stops after the target and structurally shares the untouched list suffix.

Five-repeat release sweep:

| widgets | drag median | allocated |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 0.003 ms | 13,568 B |
| 100 | 0.024 ms | 89,168 B |
| 250 | 0.063 ms | 215,168 B |
| 500 | 0.237 ms | 425,168 B |
| 1,000 | 0.550 ms | 773,240 B |

The deterministic regression rejects 1,000-widget drag allocation at or above
2,000,000 bytes and rejects a 100-to-1,000 allocation ratio at or above 15.
It also requires unchanged scene and setter calls to preserve physical identity.
